Lipreadle

Watch the mouth. Guess the five-letter word. As many tries as you need.

    How to play

    Someone says a five-letter word, silently. Guess it.

    Each miss tells you how alike the two words look on the lips, from nothing like it to looks the same. Never anything about letters.

    Tap the clip to replay it. Half speed helps.

    Guess until you get it; fewer is better. A new word arrives at midnight, your time.
